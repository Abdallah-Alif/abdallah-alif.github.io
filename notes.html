Projectile Dynamics Research Notes

1. Notation

  • r(t): position vector
  • v(t): velocity
  • m: mass
  • A: cross-sectional area
  • C_D: drag coefficient
  • Ω: Earth's angular velocity vector
  • R_E: Earth radius
  • ρ_a(h): atmospheric density at altitude h
  • ρ_p: projectile density

2. Core Equation & Its Omitted Terms

Ideal equation

$$ m \, \ddot{r} = - m g \hat{y} $$

Complete physical equation (classical)

$$ m \, \ddot{r} = - m g(h) \hat{r} + F_a + F_m + F_c + F_\nabla + F_s $$

Force Definitions

Aerodynamic drag:

$$ F_a = -\frac{1}{2} \rho_a C_D A |v_r| v_r $$

Magnus lift (simplified):

$$ F_m = \frac{1}{2} \rho_a C_L A |\omega| (\omega \times v_r) $$

Coriolis + centrifugal:

$$ F_c = - 2 m (\Omega \times \dot{r}) - m (\Omega \times (\Omega \times r)) $$

Altitude-dependent gravity:

$$ g(h) = g_0 \left( \frac{R_E}{R_E + h} \right)^2, \quad h = y \text{ (for flat approximation)} $$

Tidal and stochastic forces:

$$ F_\nabla = - m (\nabla \Phi_\text{tidal}) \quad \text{(neglected)} $$ $$ F_s \quad \text{(stochastic turbulence forces)} $$

3. Order-of-Magnitude Analysis for Dominant Perturbations

3.1 Aerodynamic Drag Breakdown

Drag-to-weight ratio:

$$ \epsilon_d = \frac{F_d}{mg} = \frac{\rho_a C_D A v^2}{2 m g} $$

For a sphere:

$$ \epsilon_d = \frac{3}{4} \frac{\rho_a}{\rho_p} \frac{C_D v^2}{g d} $$

Failure onset: $$ \epsilon_d \gtrsim 0.01 $$

Example: $$ \rho_p / \rho_a \sim 10^3, \, d \sim 0.01 \, m \implies v \gtrsim 30 \, \text{m/s} $$

Drag coefficient dependency:

$$ C_D \approx \frac{24}{Re} \left( 1 + 0.15 Re^{0.687} \right), \quad Re < 800 $$ $$ Re = \frac{\rho_a v d}{\mu} $$

Transonic issues near $$ Ma \approx 0.6 $$

3.2 Coriolis Deviation

Horizontal acceleration magnitude:

$$ a_c \sim 2 \Omega v \sin\phi $$

Lateral displacement over flight time \( t_f \):

$$ \delta x_c \sim \frac{1}{3} \Omega \sin\phi \, v_x t_f^2 $$

Example: $$ v_x = 200 \, \text{m/s}, t_f = 50\,\text{s}, \phi = 45^\circ \implies \delta x_c \sim 8.6\,\text{m} $$

3.3 Altitude-Dependent Gravity & Curvature

Vertical error from constant gravity assumption:

$$ \delta y_g \sim \frac{1}{3} g_0 \frac{t^3 v_{y0}}{R_E} $$

Example: $$ v_{y0} \sim 500 \, \text{m/s}, t = 100\,\text{s} \implies \delta y_g \sim 1.1\,\text{m} $$

Earth curvature effect over horizontal range \( R \):

$$ \delta y_R \approx \frac{R^2}{2 R_E} $$

Example: $$ R = 30 \, \text{km} \implies \delta y_R \approx 70\,\text{m} $$

3.4 Magnus Effect for Spinning Projectiles

Simplified transverse force:

$$ F_M = S (\omega \times v_r), \quad S \approx \frac{\pi}{8} \rho_a d^3 C_M $$

Spin parameter: $$ S_p = \frac{2 v \omega d}{1} $$

Example: baseball $$ d = 0.074\,\text{m}, \omega \sim 200 \,\text{rad/s}, v = 40\,\text{m/s} \implies F_M \sim 0.4\,\text{N} $$

4. Coupled Dynamics

When drag and Magnus both present:

$$ m \dot{v} = - m g \hat{y} - \frac{1}{2} \rho_a A C_D |v_r| v_r + S (\omega \times v_r) $$

Spin decay:

$$ I \dot{\omega} = - \kappa \rho_a d^5 |\omega| \omega $$

Notes: System is coupled nonlinear ODEs. Separation of timescales can be applied if $$ \tau_\omega \gg t_f $$, otherwise likely chaotic for some initial conditions.

5. Unresolved Technical Questions

  • Transonic Drag Parameterization: Can \( C_D(Ma, Re) \) be expressed as a universal function like \( Ma \cdot Re^{1/2} \) for non-spherical shapes?
  • Stochastic Atmospheric Modeling: For wind fluctuations \( \delta u(r,t) \) with spectrum \( E(k) \), what is \( \langle \delta r^2(t) \rangle \)? Can a Fokker-Planck approximation be used?
  • Boundary-Layer Interaction: Does rapid spin lower Re for turbulent transition? How does it affect \( C_M(S_p) \)?
  • Minimal Model for Bifurcations: Are there Hopf bifurcations producing oscillatory trajectories in 6D phase-space?
  • High-Altitude Rarefied Flow: For \( Kn > 0.1 \), can DSMC drag be approximated as \( F_d = -\frac{1}{2} \rho_a A v^2 [ C_D(0) + C_D(1) \cdot Kn + \dots ] \)?
  • Numerical Sensitivity: Does Lyapunov exponent scale as \( \lambda \sim v^\alpha d^\beta \) for deterministic prediction horizon?
